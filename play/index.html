Dots that give their lives: playground

presentationsource

Two worlds run side by side from the same start. A dot can give all its energy to a neighbour and die; on the left the energy goes to the neighbour it met (usually a relative), on the right to a random dot anywhere (a stranger). Change the world on the left panel, restart, and see whether self-sacrifice still evolves.
